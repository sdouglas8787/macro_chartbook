The numbers

Latest and prior month are annual changes. Wtd-R3 and Wtd-R12 weight the last three and twelve annual changes linearly toward the most recent month (Wtd-R3 weights 1-3 of 6, the latest carries 50%; Wtd-R12 weights 1-12 of 78, the latest carries 15.4%). Rolling 12M is unweighted: the last twelve months' average against the prior twelve - when it diverges from Wtd-R12, the series has turned. Wtd-R12 fwd is a projection, not a forecast: the next twelve months' average against the last twelve IF the index keeps moving at the Wtd-R12 pace. CAGR compounds the index over two, three and five years. All computed from index levels; the method, worked through, is in the README.
